# AI-Powered Study & Flashcard Platform

An adaptive, AI-driven learning and study application designed to help students optimize their learning through intelligent flashcard generation, custom notes, interactive study plans, gamified virtual environments, and custom themes.

---

## Key Features

### 📅 Adaptive Learning & AI Study Plans
* **Dynamic Placement (Adaptive Test):** The app begins by using AI to ask questions with varying difficulties.
* **Guided Study Sessions:** Compiles your chosen study sets to facilitate continuous, focused learning.

### AI-Generated Decks & Lessons

* **Automated Flashcard Creation:** The AI can scan your existing notes or manual flashcards to instantly generate new cards, minimizing manual input.
* **Custom Quiz Generation:** Take comprehensive, guided quizzes sourced straight from your personal notes or flashcard collections to test your overall knowledge retention.

### Expanded Question Formats

The AI seamlessly supports and generates cards across multiple specialized formats:

* **Multiple Choice (MCQ):** Standard single-choice questions with AI-generated distractors (Max 4 options).
* **Multi-Select Checkboxes:** Advanced "Select all that apply" questions where the user chooses multiple correct answers, and the AI generates the remaining options (Max 4 options).
* **True or False:** Binary-choice questions for fast-paced knowledge verification.
* **Written Response:** Traditional text-based questions requiring typed answers to evaluate deeper conceptual understanding.

### Smart Note-Taking & Organization

* **Flexible Text Editor:** Rich text capabilities including standard formatting (Bold, Italic, Underline), font sizing, and external document importing.
* **Seamless Folder Management:** Quickly categorize notes into subjects or topics directly from the creation view using the dedicated folder symbol at the top.
* **Smart Organization Suggestions:** When creating multiple independent notes, the AI will suggest grouping them into an appropriate folder.
* **AI Enhancement:** Refine your notes with AI-generated suggestions, allowing you to edit, accept, or discard changes freely.

### Analytics & Gamification

* **Detailed Reports:** Features a matrix that converts your active study time into a performance score.
* **Consistency Trackers:** Track your daily study streak to maintain momentum.
* **Competitive Leaderboards:** Rank up against other students to stay motivated.
